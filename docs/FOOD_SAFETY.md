# Food safety and responsible sharing

ShareMeal coordinates information and a local handoff. It does not inspect, test, certify, store, transport or guarantee food. Donor-submitted details do not prove safety.

Listings collect optional food classification, preparation time, use-by time, storage conditions, known allergens, packaging condition and handling instructions. Collection deadlines and use-by values are checked for date ordering, but the platform does not invent a universal safe-consumption window.

Donors should list only food they are lawfully able to donate, handle/store it appropriately, report known allergen and packaging details accurately and choose a realistic collection deadline. NGOs should independently assess food and transport/storage suitability at handoff, decline uncertain items and follow local authority guidance. A volunteer pickup confirmation is not a food-safety inspection.

Rules vary with jurisdiction and food type. Consult the local food-safety authority. This platform content is not legal or food-safety advice.
